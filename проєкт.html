CINNAMON ROLLS

INGREDIENTS

  • Flour 500g
  • Milk 250ml
  • Sugar 50g
  • Dry yeast 7g
  • Egg 1pc
  • Butter 50g
  • Sour cream 300g
  • Cinnamon 1tbs
  • Mix warm milk, suggar, yeast, and a pinch of sault
  • Leave for 30mins
  • Add flour, egg and butter
  • Make dough and leave it for 1 hour
  • Roll out the dough, spread some cinnamon and sugar
  • Form your cinnamon rolls and bake the for 35/180 approximately
  • Whip sour cream and spread over hot buns
  • Leave them in the oven for 5 more mins
  • Take them out and let the cool

    • Bon apetit

CINNAMON ROLLS

Ingredients

  • Flour 500g
  • Milk 250ml
  • Sugar 50g
  • Dry yeast 7g
  • Egg 1 pc
  • Butter 50g
  • Sour cream 300g
  • Cinnamon 1 tbsp

Preparation

  1. Mix warm milk, sugar, yeast, and a pinch of salt.
  2. Leave for 30 minutes.
  3. Add flour, egg, and butter.
  4. Make dough and leave it for 1 hour.
  5. Roll out the dough, spread some cinnamon and sugar.
  6. Form your cinnamon rolls and bake them for 35 min at 180°C approximately.
  7. Whip sour cream and spread over hot buns.
  8. Leave them in the oven for 5 more minutes.
  9. Take them out and let them cool.
Bon appétit! 🥐
